frequently asked question

what is StreamNest?

StreamNest is a streaming service that offers a wide variety of award-winning movies, TV shows, and documentaries on thousands of internet-connected devices.

How much does StreamNest cost?

Watch StreamNest on your smartphone, tablet, smart TV, laptop, or streaming device — all for one fixed monthly fee. Plans range from Rs 250 to Rs 850 a month, with no extra costs or contracts.

Where can I watch?

Watch anywhere, anytime, on an unlimited number of devices. Sign in with your account to watch instantly on the web at streamnest.com, or on any internet-connected device.

How do I cancel?

StreamNest is flexible. There are no annoying contracts and no commitments. You can easily cancel your account online in two clicks. There are no cancellation fees — start or stop your account anytime.

Is StreamNest good for kids?

Yes. The StreamNest Kids experience is included in your membership, letting parents control what their children watch with a PIN, while kids enjoy family-friendly TV shows and movies in their own space.
